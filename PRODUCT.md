# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primarily the developer, then friends and family, with potential to grow to a broader golfer audience. Primary use context is on the course between holes, on a phone. Secondary contexts: start and end of a round, post-round review, and using the AI caddy on the range or in practice.

## Product Purpose

A personal golf round tracker that captures hole-by-hole data (score, fairways hit, GIR, putts, miss direction) and delivers AI-powered analysis that acts like a knowledgeable caddy. Not a generic stats dump — a sharp read on tendencies and what to work on. Success looks like: the app disappears into the round, and the AI analysis is the payoff you look forward to after.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, easy to use, accurate. Warm but not soft. Data-forward without being overwhelming. Feels like a tool built by someone who plays the game, not a product team that studied it.

## Anti-references

No explicit anti-references, but the north star is the opposite of a pain in the ass: no hunting for what to tap, no dead ends, no screens that require reading before acting. Avoid the cluttered, feature-heavy feel that makes some golf apps exhausting.

## Design Principles

1. **Glanceable first** — every screen communicates its core content in seconds; the user is between holes, not at a desk
2. **Friction is the enemy** — obvious actions, fast paths, nothing that makes the user stop and think about the app instead of their game
3. **Data serves the story** — show what helps, hide what doesn't; the AI caddy analysis is the payoff, not the scoreboard
4. **Personal before polished** — warmth over corporate shine; this started as one person's tool and should feel that way
5. **Confident, not clever** — sharp and accurate; no gimmicks, no decorative complexity

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Mobile-first — the primary surface is a phone screen outdoors in sunlight, so contrast and tap target size matter more than usual. No known specific user needs beyond that.
